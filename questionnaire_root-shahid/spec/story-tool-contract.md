# STORY TOOL CONTRACT (v1)

## PURPOSE

Defines structured input format for narrative construction system.

This system receives structured intelligence from the Intake System.

---

## INPUT DATA SCOPE

The Story Tool receives ONLY:

- audience
- perception
- differentiation_signals
- story_tool_payload

---

## DATA CONTRACT

### AUDIENCE
- primary audience
- secondary audience
- pressures
- emotional needs
- objections
- trust drivers

---

### PERCEPTION
- current perception
- misconceptions
- desired shift

---

### DIFFERENTIATION SIGNALS
- capabilities
- behaviors
- beliefs
- experience patterns

---

### STORY TOOL PAYLOAD
- audience tensions
- narrative inputs
- proof architecture
- language patterns
- hooks
- conflicts

---

## RULES

- No raw unstructured text allowed
- No duplicate narrative representations
- No full questionnaire export
- Only structured fields permitted

---

## DESIGN INTENT

Ensures the Story Tool operates only on:
> structured strategic intelligence, not raw conversation data
